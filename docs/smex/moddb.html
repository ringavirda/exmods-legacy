An industrial-era iron and steel production chain for Vintage Story, built as a set of multiblock structures and network blocks. It adds horizontal progression past the vanilla cementation furnace: you smelt iron in bulk in a blast furnace (Stage I), then refine it into steel in a Bessemer converter (Stage II). Gas pipes move air, blast and exhaust between the machines, and molten canals plumb the liquid metal to molds and bulk storage.

How each structure and block works, with every number behind it, is on the Steelmaking Expanded wiki.

Compatibility

  • Game version: built for Vintage Story 1.22. Builds for 1.21 and 1.20 are on the GitHub releases page.
  • Multiplayer: works in singleplayer and on dedicated servers.
  • Requires: Expanded Library (exlib) 0.8.4 or later and Pipes and Power Expanded (ppex) 0.7.1 or later, each in the build for your game version. Install both before updating.
  • Languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
  • Save safety: safe to add to an existing world. Removing it leaves the placed machine blocks behind, so break them first if you uninstall. Back up a world before updating to 0.10.1: it cannot go back to 0.9.9 or earlier.
  • Successors: Iron Industry Expanded and Steel Industry Expanded. With either installed in the same world, this mod and Pipes and Power Expanded close there: what is built keeps working and nothing new can be built. See Compatibility.

Quick start

The iron line, in order:

  1. Set up the feed: crushed iron ore, lime, and coke or charcoal.
  2. Build the blast furnace around its door, with the Reinforced Hopper and the Bell Hopper on top of the shaft. Hold ctrl and shift and right-click the door to show the build outline. Stock the hopper.
  3. Give it air: a Twin-Tub Blower on a waterwheel's axle, or an Air Blower behind a Pipes and Power Expanded steam engine, piped to both tuyeres. Pipe the furnace's gas outlets to a Smoke Stack.
  4. Light the charge with a torch and shut the door. Once it melts, open the iron tap into a canal run that ends on mold pedestals, and tap the slag from the other side.
  5. Then add a pair of Cowper stoves for hot blast, and a Bessemer converter for steel.

What is in the mod

The blast furnace

A tall brick tower fed from hoppers on top, blown through two tuyeres and drained through an iron tap and a slag tap. It smelts crushed ore in bulk into a reservoir of molten iron, and runs on cold blast from a waterwheel-driven blower, so a furnace makes iron before there is any steam power. Blast furnace, Ironmaking

Blowers

The Twin-Tub Blower runs on any axle you already turn. The Air Blower is a sub-machine for a Pipes and Power Expanded steam engine and reaches the higher pressure the converter needs. Ironmaking

Hot blast

A pair of Cowper stoves triples the furnace's output: one charges on the furnace's exhaust while the other blows preheated air into the tuyeres, and valves swap them over. The Smoke Stack vents the exhaust and keeps the furnace drawing. Hot blast, Cowper stove, Smoke stack

Molten metal

Canals and taps carry the melt downhill from the furnace to where you want it, barrels hold it, and it cools and freezes in a run left standing. Mold pedestals and tool molds for plates, ingots and rods cast it, and slag tapped off freezes and chips into fertilizer, mortar and paving. Molten metal, Casting, Slag and its products

The Bessemer converter

A vessel that blows molten iron into steel. It takes high-pressure blast from a steam blower, an axle to tilt it, a canal in and a canal out, and cold scrap with the iron if you have it. Bessemer converter, Bessemer process

For players and server owners

Point at any machine to read its live status. .exmod network hi colours each pipe network, gauges read in metric or imperial, every gameplay value can be changed live with /exmod config smex, the tool molds can be switched off with /exmod molds, and a cheap recipe level lowers the build cost for a lighter game.
