Have you ever wanted to watch machines work and steam boil? This mod adds steam power to Vintage Story: boilers that turn coal and water into steam, engines that turn steam into mechanical power, and the pipes that carry it all between them.

The pipe network was originally built to move gases around the Blast Furnace from my other mod Steelmaking Expanded. Since the piping and steam power stand well on their own, I split them into this mod and reworked them to be more immersive.

How each block works, with every number behind it, is on the Pipes and Power Expanded wiki.

Compatibility

  • Game version: built for Vintage Story 1.22. Builds for 1.21 and 1.20 are on the GitHub releases page.
  • Multiplayer: works in singleplayer and on dedicated servers.
  • Requires: Expanded Library (exlib) 0.8.4 or later, in the build for your game version.
  • Languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
  • Save safety: safe to add to an existing world. Removing it leaves the placed machine and pipe blocks behind, so break them first if you uninstall. Back up a world before updating to 0.7.1: it cannot go back to 0.6.9 or earlier.
  • Successors: Iron Industry Expanded and Steel Industry Expanded. With either installed in the same world, this mod and Steelmaking Expanded close there: what is built keeps working and nothing new can be built. See Compatibility.

Quick start

The smallest steam plant that keeps itself running:

  1. Set a Fluid Intake on a pond, over a full three-deep cube of water.
  2. Build a Cornish Boiler, fill it through its lid with buckets of water, and stand a chimney on its exhaust outlet before you light the fire.
  3. Pipe its steam to a Watt Engine, with a Pressure Valve hung off a T-junction in that line and gated at 3.5 atm.
  4. Set a Fluid Pump in the engine's drive cell and pipe the intake through it into the boiler's feed, with a second Pressure Valve on the feed line gated at 0.5 atm to spill what the boiler does not take.

Getting started walks through each step.

What is in the mod

Pipe networks

Iron and steel pipes, bends and junctions join into networks that carry air, exhaust, steam or water under pressure. Gases compress and push on, water moves only where a pump drives it, an open end leaks, and a pipe pushed past its limit bursts. .exmod network hi colours each network so you can see where a run is joined and where it is broken. Pipes and pressure

Valves and fittings

A hand valve cuts a run, a pressure valve holds a machine's feed at the pressure it needs, a steam condenser turns steam back into feed water, and outlets and passthroughs carry a run through a wall or into a machine. A fluid intake draws water from a pond. Valves and outlets

Boilers

Two coal-fired boilers, each a structure you build stage by stage around its vessel. The Cornish Boiler is the iron-age entry; the Lancashire Boiler is the steel tier with the pressure the bigger engine wants. A boiler needs water, a fire and a chimney to draw it, and one held at its top pressure with the fire lit explodes. Boilers

Steam engines

The Watt Engine runs on low-pressure steam; the Cornish Engine wants high pressure and can be throttled up or down. An engine does its work through the sub-machine placed in its drive cell: a Fluid Pump, a Mechanical Power Generator that drives axles, or the air blower from Steelmaking Expanded. Every engine returns part of its steam as condensate, so a plant can feed itself. Steam engines

Pumps

A hand-cranked Manual Fluid Pump and an axle-driven Mechanical Fluid Pump move water before any engine runs, enough to fill a first boiler. Mechanical power and pumps

For players and server owners

Gauges read in metric or imperial (.exmod measure), every gameplay value can be changed live with /exmod config ppex, and a cheap recipe level lowers the build cost for a lighter game. Commands and config
